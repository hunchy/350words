The was nothing subtle about it but I was one mouse click away from losing USD 2,213.46.

I didn't click. So I lost nothing but my card was compromised. Its details are probably now in the hands of an army of dark web operatives. I hope cancelling it and ordering a new one will be enough.

Here's how it unfolded.

1. I got a WhatsApp message asking me to confirm a hotel booking and to click a link to access their online confirmation form.
2. The booking was legitimate so no flags raised.
3. I filled out the form that included, amongst other things, my card details (call me stupid, I won't disagree). But weirdly, some of the pre-filled fields were correct.
4. I immediately received an SMS from my bank asking me to confirm transfer of USD 2,213.46 to uzairways.com. This where I exited the transaction.
5. There were some more exhortations to complete the 'validation process' but by this time, I was pretty sure what was happening.

One click away. 

Later in the day I checked the form code in a private window. ChatGPT tells me the phishing kit inside is quite adaptable, able to scam pretty much every currency and speak in any of 41 languages.

I think they're very new at the game. The form domain, booking.stayfrutured.com, was only registered yesterday (2026-09-24) in Hong Kong.

Anyway, I learned something today. The only question I have is how they got my reservation details in the first place. I really hope it's not an internal job.
