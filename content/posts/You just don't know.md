There was a crazy guy this morning in the park across the road from Moto, the café we go to for a post-surf coffee.

CG was yelling loudly and incessantly, fkn this, fkn that, everything was fkd anyway and why was the world picking on him.

The rants ran for about 5 minutes each, and in between CG walked around looking at the sky, the ground, the trees, anything.

No one went near him. We sat there drinking our coffees and thought, glad we're not in that park with him.

He was in his own private world of pain and no one wanted to join him there. Others walking through the park turned back, or walked wide to get past without him noticing.

This is the thing. We now live in a world where we know psychoses and mental health are real, not imaginary. I use the word 'crazy' above but I'm not sure now that's remotely appropriate. 

Whatever brough it on - drugs, trauma, booze, a medical issue - doesn't matter. CG is in a place that's not 'normal' by our standards and dealing with it in the only way he knows.

None of us live his life or walk in his shoes. We can't know how he perceives things, that perception is his alone.

I don't have answers. Do we treat him so he behaves by our mutually-agreed standard of 'normal,' whatever that is? Do we offer him a safe environment to live his life? Do we give him space and walk past because he seems threatening? Do we even care?
