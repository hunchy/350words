I use AI as part of my writing workflow, and it’s possible my work will get flagged as partly or fully AI-generated by whatever the detector-of-the-day is.

I don’t care.

My goal is to provide value to those who read my work, not to virtue-signal my originality.

AI helps me to research a topic, find supporting or contrarian ideas, locate gaps in my reasoning, edit my drafts, and correct my less-than-stellar grammar.

The finished article is my work. I take full responsibility for its errors, omissions, judgements, recommendations and conclusions. Each article reflects my genuine understanding of the topic at the time I publish it. That understanding can, and likely will, change over time.
