Maybe it's just me and my cynicism, but the posts I see on social and particularly LinkedIn are mostly tedious and boring. There's very little I see, as I doom scroll down, that makes me stop and say 'wow.'

The thing is the posts aren't bad. They tell me something I could, or should, find interesting.

But they don't change my thinking. They give me nothing to process. There is no 'aha' moment.

I have an artsy friend I surf with. He says that you know good art because after you've seen it and absorbed it, you've changed. I think he's onto something there. I can go through an art gallery and look at famous paintings. Most say nothing to me. They are pretty pictures I enjoy in the moment but when I move on, I'm the same person.

But a few do make an impact. I wish I could explain it, and distil it, and use it in my own work.

Too often I try to be flashy with my words and sentence structure. Like slowly opening the kimono.
Or I try to be clever and unique in the way I develop a concept.

More and more, I think that's wrong. Maybe I just should say it plain, and explain why it matters, and why you (you, the reader) would be dumb to think anything else.

Because every reader makes an instant decision when a post comes into view: What's here for me?

And if nothing leaps off the screen and into their head: I've failed.

Here are 4 models I found that I will be trying out:

1. I thought X -> I noticed Y -> So now I think Z.
2. Tension -> Explanation -> Consequence.
3. Everyone does X -> Here's what really happens -> Therefore try Z.
4. Here's something weird -> Here's why it happens -> Here's what it means for you.
