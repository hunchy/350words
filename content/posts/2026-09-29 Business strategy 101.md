Every function in your business does one of these three things:

1. It creates revenue
2. It enables revenue
3. It consumes revenue.

Simple but uncommon perspective. All are necessary but only one brings home the bacon.

These three things define every business strategy ever:

1. Do as much of (1) as you can afford
2. Do (2) for as little cost as possible without compromising the expected quality or funtionality
3. Do as little of (3) as you can get away with and still achieve (1) and (2).

That's it. Everything else in business is tactics.
